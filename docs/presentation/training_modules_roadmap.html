Notebook: Module 3Model and scalar inventory
Rules that do not change between modules

Reproducibility depends on a small set of scientific invariants.

01
PM2.5 validityGround records are retained only from 1 through 1000 µg/m³, with screening documented.
02
Complete three-hour meansEach UTC interval requires exactly three hourly observations: n_obs = 3.
03
Midpoint timestamps[00:00,03:00) is represented at 01:30 UTC, then every three hours.
04
Spatial evidenceNearest-grid matching uses Haversine distance; station, grid center, row, column, and distance are retained.
05
Forecast identityInitialization time, valid time, and forecast day remain separate fields.
06
Model reproducibilityStatic global min-max values, saved splits, feature order, and model version are reused at inference.
Quality questionCould another analyst trace a plotted point back to its monitor record, GEOS file, grid cell, valid time, and model version?
